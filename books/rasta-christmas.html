Rasta Christmas

Rasta Christmas and the Santa Corps: The Summer Connection

By Glenn Beavers

About this book

On a beautiful Caribbean island, best friends Alesha and Jaden set out to investigate the mysterious Nightsurfer for a school competition. But their adventure takes an unexpected turn when they become lost near an island volcano.

Rescued by Rasta Christmas, a remarkable member of the secret Santa Corps, they discover a magical world of extraordinary characters, strange discoveries and unexpected friendships. When a powerful hurricane threatens the island and Alesha’s father is caught at sea, the adventure becomes a race against time.

A colourful fantasy filled with friendship, courage, humour and Christmas magic that lasts all year round.

Promotional description drafted for Glenn’s review before publication.
